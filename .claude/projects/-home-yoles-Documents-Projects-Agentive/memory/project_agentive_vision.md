**Agentive** is a self-hosted web interface for designing, deploying, and operating AI-powered departments/companies.

**Key facts:**
- Primary purpose: internal tool for John's agency to create AI departments (legal, fiscal, SEO, dev, etc.)
- NOT a SaaS — self-hosted, local-first
- The SEO use case in the docs is an illustration, not the sole focus
- The Company Builder (meta-agent that designs AI companies) is the core differentiator
- The existing documents (prd_x_brainstorm.md, prd_x_brainstorm_v2.md, rapport_outils_agent_ia.md, workflow_agent_ia.md) are partial brainstorms and directional documents, NOT finalized PRDs

**Why:** John needs custom, customizable AI teams for his agency's various departments. Productization is secondary.

**How to apply:** Always frame suggestions around John's agency needs first. Don't over-optimize for hypothetical external users. Focus on practical, buildable architecture.
